01 // Features

Everything due, in one place.

Instructors post deadlines inside long messages, in English, Arabic or both. Blackboarder finds them and keeps them organised across all your devices.

01.01 // FINDING DEADLINES

Reads announcements for you

Click Scan on Blackboard and a built-in parser picks out quizzes, assignments, exams, dates and times from English and Arabic posts, right in your browser. For long or vague announcements you can add an optional AI key.

Quiz / كويز Assignment / واجب Exam / امتحان Optional AI
01.02 // CALENDAR

A calendar that stays in order

Monday-first month view and a deadline list. Each day is sorted by time, and edits re-sort straight away.

Month view Day details
01.03 // GRADES

Marks and grade weights

See how much each deliverable counts, such as a 25% midterm or 10% lab report. Every scan reads your Blackboard gradebook and puts each mark on its deadline, grouped by course with the newest first, plus how much of each syllabus is done.

Marks Syllabus progress Syllabus weights Rooms & times
01.04 // SYNC

Private sync, no account

Every install gets its own random sync key, BBS- plus 10 characters. Your deadlines sync through Firebase under that key, and each key can only reach its own data. No sign-up, no password, nothing tied to your Blackboard login.

Laptop ⇄ Phone Isolated per key No sign-up
01.05 // PHONE

Phone app

The same calendar on your phone. Add it to your home screen on iPhone or Android, use it offline, get reminders before each deadline even with the app closed, and open marks, attendance and your study plan from quick links.

iPhone / Android Works offline Reminders
01.06 // CALENDAR FEED

Deadlines in your calendar app

Subscribe once in Apple Calendar, Google Calendar or Outlook. New and changed deadlines show up there automatically.

Apple Google Outlook
01.07 // UPDATES

Updates itself

No Web Store needed. After a one-time setup the extension checks GitHub every 15 minutes and installs new versions on its own, keeping your tasks and settings.

15-minute check Data kept
01.08 // COMFORT

Side panel and dark mode

Keep Blackboarder open next to Blackboard in the browser side panel, and switch the extension, the phone app and Blackboard itself to dark mode.

Side panel Dark Blackboard Alt+B
02 // Install

Set up in about two minutes.

Works in Chrome, Edge, Brave, Opera and Vivaldi on Windows, macOS and Linux. The extension is already built; there is nothing to compile.

01

Download

Download the ZIP and unzip it somewhere permanent, such as Documents. Keep the folder; the browser loads the extension from it.

Download Extension ZIP →
02

Load it

Open chrome://extensions (or edge://, brave://), turn on Developer mode, click Load unpacked and choose the unzipped folder (or dist if using git).

03

Turn on updates

Double-click update.bat in the folder (Windows) or run ./update.sh (macOS, Linux). Then click the reload arrow on Blackboarder once.

04

Scan Blackboard

Open Blackboard and click the floating Scan Blackboard button, or press Alt+B (Option+Shift+B on Mac) to open Blackboarder.

Using git instead? Clone the repository and load its dist folder the same way. The updater then runs git pull for you, but only when your clone is on main with no uncommitted changes, so it never touches your work.

03 // Phone & Calendar

Take it with you.

Pair your phone with one link, then subscribe your calendar app to your deadlines.

Pair your phone

  1. In the extension, open Settings and click Copy Phone Pairing Link.
  2. Open the link on your phone. The app pairs with your key and loads your deadlines.
  3. Add it to your home screen: Share > Add to Home Screen on iPhone, Install app in Chrome on Android.
Blackboarder Synced

Calculus 1 Midterm Mon 12 Oct // 10:00 AM // W8-102

Subscribe your calendar

  1. In Settings click Copy WebCal Feed Link, or tap Subscribe in the phone app.
  2. Apple Calendar: open the link and confirm the subscription.
  3. Google Calendar: on a computer, go to Other calendars > + > From URL and paste the link.
webcal://blackboarder.pages.dev/feed.ics?key=BBS-XXXXXXXXXX

The link contains your sync key, so keep it to yourself. If an older subscription stops updating, copy the link again: feeds now always need a key.

05 // FAQ

Questions.

Yes. Blackboarder is free and open source under the MIT License. No subscriptions, no accounts, no paid features.
It is tuned for UOS course codes, schedules and syllabus weights, but the deadline finder works on any Blackboard Learn or Ultra site.
Your tasks, courses and quick links are stored in Firebase under your sync key so your phone can read them. Your Blackboard password and cookies are never sent anywhere. Anyone who has your key or calendar link can see your deadlines, so keep them private.
No. Without a key the built-in parser reads announcements automatically. An AI key (OpenRouter, IFM or your own provider) set in the extension only helps with long or vague posts, and the phone app uses the same model through your server.
Running update.bat or update.sh once registers a small helper. The extension then checks GitHub every 15 minutes, the helper downloads the new version into your folder, and the extension restarts into it once its popup is closed. If the helper is not set up, Blackboarder shows a banner when an update is available.
