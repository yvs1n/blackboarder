How to subscribe on iPhone (Apple Calendar)
  1. Tap Subscribe in Apple Calendar above (or copy the feed URL).
  2. iOS will prompt: "Subscribe to calendar?". Tap Subscribe.
  3. Set Auto-Refresh to Every Hour and tap Add.
  4. All due dates and class times will appear natively on your iOS Calendar and Lock Screen widgets.
How to subscribe on Android / Google Calendar
  1. Copy the Calendar Feed URL using the button above.
  2. Open calendar.google.com in your browser (or desktop).
  3. Beside "Other calendars" on the left, click the + button and choose "From URL".
  4. Paste the copied URL and click Add calendar.

How to setup on iPhone (Scriptable)
  1. Download Scriptable from the App Store (free).
  2. Tap Copy Widget Script above (your Sync Key is automatically included).
  3. Open Scriptable, tap + to create a new script.
  4. Paste the code, rename the script to Blackboarder, and tap Done.
  5. On your iPhone Home Screen or Lock Screen, long-press to enter edit mode, tap +, search for Scriptable, and add a widget.
  6. Tap the widget to edit: select Blackboarder as the script. All deadlines, courses, rooms, and pending counters will display seamlessly even when offline without Wi-Fi!

Setup Guide

Get Started with Blackboarder

1. Install the Chrome Extension

The extension runs on your computer browser to safely parse your registered Blackboard courses and upcoming assignments.

  1. Unzip the extension: Download the latest blackboarder-extension.zip and extract it to a folder on your computer.
  2. Open Extensions: In Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge, navigate to chrome://extensions in your address bar.
  3. Enable Developer mode: Turn on the Developer mode toggle switch in the top-right corner.
  4. Load unpacked: Click Load unpacked at the top left, then select the extracted extension folder.
  5. Pin to toolbar: Click the puzzle piece icon in Chrome's toolbar and pin Blackboarder for one-click access.
Private & Secure: Blackboarder runs entirely on your device. Your Blackboard password and university login credentials never leave your browser.

2. Scan Blackboard Ultra Once

Let Blackboarder automatically read your stream to extract deadlines, calculate syllabus weightings, and map lecture rooms.

  1. Open Blackboard: Go to elearning.sharjah.ac.ae and sign in with your student account.
  2. Click "Scan Blackboard": A floating blue button will appear at the bottom right. Click it, or click the Blackboarder extension icon and select Scan Now.
  3. Automatic Processing: Blackboarder identifies your active courses (Calculus 1, Physics 1, Intro to Comp, EAP, Islamic Culture, etc.), calculates dynamic syllabus weights (e.g. 10% for Quizzes 1 of 2), and matches your class rooms.
Smart Deduplication: When professors edit or repost announcements, Blackboarder merges them automatically without cluttering your schedule with duplicates.

3. Pair Seamlessly via Extension

Keys are generated and secured entirely in your Chrome extension. You never need to type or remember codes manually.

1 In Chrome on your laptop, open the Blackboarder extension popup.
2 Go to Settings and click "Copy Phone Pairing Link" (or click "Open Mobile App").
3 Open that unique link on your phone (send it via WhatsApp, AirDrop, or email).
4 Your phone pairs instantly and securely with zero manual typing required!
Privacy Guarantee: Your sync code stays locked to your devices. Each student gets their own private cloud partition that nobody else can access.
